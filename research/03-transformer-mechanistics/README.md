# Research Track 03: Transformer Inference Dynamics & Circuit Mechanistics

## Focus
Mechanistic interpretability of autoregressive transformer execution, attention circuits, feed-forward associative recall, and computational scratchpad dynamics.

## Core Pillars
- **Residual Stream Communication Bus**: Additive communication channel across depth $L$ and the Linear Representation Hypothesis.
- **Induction Circuits ($QK$ and $OV$)**: In-context copy heads and template pattern tracking.
- **MLP Layers as Associative Key-Value Memories**: Factual retrieval, key detection, and parameter space knowledge localization.
- **Representation Engineering**: Contrastive Activation Addition (CAA), singular value decomposition (SVD) steering vectors, and Top-$k$ Sparse Autoencoders (SAEs).
- **Chain of Thought (CoT) Scratchpads**: Circuit complexity boundaries ($\text{TC}^0$ single-pass limit vs $\text{P}$-complete sequential reasoning) and KV-cache causal irreversibility.
